Common questions

Before you order

How do I share my eye prescription?

Add your preferred frame and lens option to the cart. The WhatsApp message will ask you to attach your latest prescription after sending the order.

Can I order prescription sunglasses?

Yes. Select a prescription-sunglasses product and choose the appropriate lens option. Availability depends on your power and selected frame.

How do I choose the right frame size?

Compare the frame width shown on each product with the measurements printed inside your current glasses. In-store fitting is recommended when unsure.

Are contact lenses available without a prescription?

Contact lenses should be selected using a valid prescription and professional fitting advice. Share your lens power and preferred replacement cycle on WhatsApp.
